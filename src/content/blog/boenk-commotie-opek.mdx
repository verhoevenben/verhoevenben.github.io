*Double the trouble, twice the fun.* On Saturday, November 21,
[**Commotie**](https://commotie.art/kalender/voorstelling.html?id=boenk-commotie-2026-11-21) and
**BOENK!**, the adult group of [Terboenk!](https://www.terboenk.be/), share the stage at OPEK in
Leuven. Expect lots of music, the occasional complete tangle of storylines, and at least one
intense scene between Klaas and Han. Each group throws itself fully into half of the show.

- **When:** Saturday, November 21, 2026, doors 19:30, show 20:00
- **Where:** Kleine Zaal, OPEK, Vaartkom 4, Leuven
- **Tickets:** €10, students €6

Reserve your tickets through [the booking form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdFnraM3qhkvftrCovNozw143Po17BwyD102R2JGSfqag1BDw/viewform);
