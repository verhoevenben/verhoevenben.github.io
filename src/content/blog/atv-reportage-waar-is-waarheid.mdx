<div class="lang-block-en">

Yesterday [**Waar is Waarheid?**](https://www.erlnmyr.be/waar-is-waarheid/) ("Where is Truth?")
premiered at Theater Het Klokhuis in Antwerp, and regional broadcaster **ATV** was there. They made
a news report about it: *interactive theatre as an answer to fake news*. Tine Van den Broeck and I
explain why we invite children to search for the character Truth together with us, and how they
learn along the way not to jump to conclusions.

Watch the report (in Dutch) on the ATV / Gazet van Antwerpen website:
