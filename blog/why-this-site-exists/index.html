Why this site exists

Most of what I do professionally lives at ERLNMYR — the shows, the workshops, the booking details. This site is the smaller, slower space next to it: a place for writing, for notes on improv and science communication that don’t fit into a pitch deck, and for whatever else comes up.

I’ll be adding to the about and work pages as things change, and posting here whenever there’s something worth writing down. If you’re after a show or a workshop, ERLNMYR is still the right door to knock on.
